Central government, PSU, and autonomous institute notifications express compensation, age cutoffs, and reservation eligibility in standardized administrative formats. Knowing how to read these entries prevents avoidable disqualifications during document verification.

## 1. Reading Pay Matrix Levels

Most central civil services and ministerial recruitments follow the **7th Central Pay Commission (CPC) Pay Matrix**:

| Pay Level | Entry Basic Pay | Typical Cadre Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Level 2 | ₹19,900 | Junior Clerk cum Typist, DRDO Technician 'A' |
| Level 3 | ₹21,700 | Commercial cum Ticket Clerk |
| Level 4 | ₹25,500 | Tax Assistant (CBDT / CBIC) |
| Level 5 | ₹29,200 | Auditor, Accountant (CAG / CGDA) |
| Level 6 | ₹35,400 | Junior Statistical Officer |
| Level 7 | ₹44,900 | Assistant Section Officer, Inspector, AIIMS Nursing Officer |
| Level 10 | ₹56,100 | UPSC Engineering Services, ISRO Scientist / Engineer 'SC' |

In addition to basic pay, regular employees receive Dearness Allowance (DA), House Rent Allowance (HRA based on X/Y/Z city classification), and Transport Allowance as notified from time to time.

## 2. Crucial Date for Age Limit & Educational Qualifications

Every recruitment notice specifies a **crucial date** (often either the closing date of applications or January 1 / August 1 of the examination year):

- **Age Limit**: Your date of birth must fall strictly within the notified window on the crucial date, unless eligible for statutory upper-age relaxation.
- **Educational Result Declaration**: Final-year candidates are eligible only if their final degree or diploma result is officially declared on or before the crucial date specified in the circular. Keep provisional certificates and consolidated mark sheets ready before applying.

## 3. Format Validity for Category & EWS Certificates

Reservation claims under OBC (Non-Creamy Layer), SC, ST, EWS, or PwBD require certificates issued in the prescribed central government proforma:

- **OBC-NCL & EWS Certificates**: Agencies typically require certificates valid for the current financial year (FY 2026–27) based on income/wealth assessment of the preceding financial year, issued prior to the crucial cutoff date.
- **Name & Date Consistency**: Ensure your name, parent's name, and date of birth match across your Class 10 certificate, government photo ID, OTR profile, and category certificate before locking your application.
