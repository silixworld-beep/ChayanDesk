Lookalike recruitment websites routinely mirror official commission branding during peak application cycles such as SSC CGL, RRB NTPC, and India Post GDS drives. Before entering personal identity numbers or initiating a fee payment, confirm every link against the issuing agency's authoritative domain.

## 1. Check the Exact Domain Authority

Central government ministries, commissions, and statutory institutions publish recruitment notices exclusively on verified government and statutory top-level domains:

- **`.gov.in` and `.nic.in`**: Central ministries, UPSC (`upsc.gov.in`), SSC (`ssc.gov.in`), DRDO (`drdo.gov.in`), and national recruitment portals (`upsconline.nic.in`, `rrbapply.gov.in`).
- **`.ac.in`**: Autonomous national institutes of importance such as AIIMS (`aiimsexams.ac.in`) and IITs.
- **`.org.in` / `.org`**: Statutory regulators and financial bodies such as the Reserve Bank of India (`rbi.org.in`).

Never trust hyphenated variations such as `ssc-gov-apply.com`, `upsconline-portal.in`, or `.blogspot` mirrors for form submission or fee payment.

## 2. Cross-Check the Notification Reference Code

Every authentic public-sector recruitment circular carries a formal file or advertisement number near the top of the PDF header—for example `CEN No. 06/2026` for Railway Recruitment Boards or `Advt. No. 1B/2026-27` for RBI. Confirm that:

1. The notification code inside the PDF matches the listing on the agency's official Notice Board or Careers page.
2. Key dates (opening date, closing date, and crucial eligibility date) match the tabular schedule inside the PDF.
3. Corrigendums or deadline extensions appear on the same official notice board under the same reference number.

## 3. Inspect Payment Gateways Before Paying

Official portals collect examination fees solely through recognized government or public-sector gateways (such as Bharatkosh, SBI ePay, IBPS integrated gateways, or official portal checkout endpoints) linked directly from the logged-in candidate dashboard. Red flags include:

- Requests to pay via personal UPI handles, QR codes sent over messaging apps, or unverified third-party payment links.
- Demands for "document verification fees" or "joining security deposits" prior to formal appointment.
- Promises of guaranteed selection without clearing notified CBT, written, or merit stages.
